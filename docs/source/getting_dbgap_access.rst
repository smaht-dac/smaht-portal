The SMaHT datasets were generated as part of the NIH Common Fund consortium initiative, Somatic Mosaicism across Human Tissues (SMaHT).

The SMaHT datasets are submitted under dbGaP studies (http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gap), with the study accession numbers, `phs004193 <https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/study/phs004193.v1.p1/#study>`_ for the SMaHT Benchmarking data, and `phs004194 <https://dbgap.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/study/phs004194.v1.p1/#study>`_ for the SMaHT Production data. The datasets were provided by the SMaHT Data Analysis Center (DAC) [1UM1DA058230] on behalf of the SMaHT Network.

We have released the SMaHT studies on dbGaP, and are in the process of submitting the benchmarking data and the first production data from 25 donors to dbGaP.

Protected-access data can be obtained from applying access to dbGaP.
